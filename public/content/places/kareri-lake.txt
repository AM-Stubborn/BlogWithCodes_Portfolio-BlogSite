Base was near Dharamshala / Kareri village. In January the trail turns into a proper snow walk — slower footing, colder camps, and quieter mountains than the summer season. We carried traction and warm layers, followed local guidance on snow depth, and reached the frozen lake under a hard blue sky. Peak-snow Kareri rewards patience more than speed.
