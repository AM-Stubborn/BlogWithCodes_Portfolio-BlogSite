Reached Bir by road from the Kangra side and spent time between the village and the Billing ridge. The drive up to takeoff is short but scenic; watching gliders lift off above the tea slopes is half the experience even if you are not flying. Evenings in Bir are quiet cafés, monastery walks, and Dhauladhar light on the horizon. Add your Hanuman Garh / Billing moments, whether you flew or spectated, and your favourite valley frame.
