Drove into Chopta and started the classic Tungnath trail in the morning. The stone path climbs steadily through alpine scrub to the temple, then continues another steep hour to Chandrashila. We timed the summit for clearer light and watched the ranges open in every direction. The descent felt longer on tired knees, but the route is well marked and doable in a long day if you start early.
